# ChatGPT, twice

**March 2023.** A bug in the Redis client library ChatGPT used showed some users the titles of other
users' conversations, and exposed payment details for some subscribers. Nothing to do with AI — a
classic isolation bug in a cache. What leaked was conversation history. —
[The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2023/03/openai-reveals-redis-bug-behind-chatgpt.html)

**July 2025.** An option to make a shared conversation "discoverable" put thousands of conversations,
some of them sensitive, into search engine results. OpenAI removed the option. —
[The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2025/08/01/openai_removes_chatgpt_selfdoxing_option/)

Neither was an attack on the model. The first was a store that failed to keep people apart; the
second was a setting that widened an audience and was not treated as a security decision.

Both are about the same thing: **history is data, and who reads it is decided somewhere.** Find
where, in this lab, and who can change it.
